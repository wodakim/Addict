# Prompts d'assets - events

Objectif: produire les illustrations d'événements utilisées par les IDs dans `assets/events/*.json`.

## Spécifications communes
- Type d'asset: event card art (icône/illustration carrée).
- Format: PNG transparent, 1024x1024.
- Composition: sujet central clair + éléments secondaires en couronne.
- Style: cartoon premium, 2.5D, cozy coffee game.

---

## event-festival-chocolat-chaud (`festival-chocolat-chaud`)
**Prompt IA image**
"A premium cozy mobile game event icon for 'Hot Chocolate Festival': a ceramic mug filled with rich dark hot chocolate, thick creamy swirl topping, cocoa powder dust, mini marshmallows, cinnamon stick, warm steam curls forming heart shapes, festive fairy lights bokeh in the background, soft pastel brown and cream palette with gold accents, stylized 2.5D cartoon rendering, clean silhouette, centered composition, transparent background, high readability at small size"

**Negative prompt**
"realistic photo, text, logo, watermark, cluttered background, harsh contrast, desaturated colors, ugly foam"
